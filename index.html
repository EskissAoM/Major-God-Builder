AoM:R Major God Builder

Create a custom major god by choosing a pantheon, god power, unique technology, bonuses, and minor gods, then export a ready-to-install mod ZIP.

1. Major god

Greek-only choices

Choose one hero for each age from that age's Greek hero pool, plus one Fortress unique unit.

Chinese-only choices

Choose one Mythic Age special hero.

Aztec-only choices

Choose one Classical Teixiptla Form and one Mythic Incarnate choice.

2. Unique technologies

Add up to two optional major-god unique technologies. The list is filtered by the selected pantheon, with special availability rules where needed.

3. God bonuses

Add up to ten optional major-god bonuses.

4. Minor gods

Choose minor gods for each age. Original minor gods are restricted to the selected pantheon; Custom minor gods can mix powers, myth units, and technologies freely. Custom minor myth units and technologies are optional and expandable.

Custom minor gods export their age techs, selected powers, units, technologies, and optional portrait/icon images.

5. Export

How to install your mod

  1. Generate the mod ZIP.
  2. Unzip the folder.
  3. Go to C:\Users\UserName\Games\Age of Mythology Retold\SteamID\mods\local.
  4. Drop the unzipped mod folder there — final path: mods\local\MyCustomMajorGod\game.
  5. Start the game and enjoy!
